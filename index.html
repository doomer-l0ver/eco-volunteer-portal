Eco-Tips

Small habits that make recycling easier.

  • Rinse, then sort

    A quick rinse of bottles and jars keeps paper and glass clean for recycling.

  • Flatten and save space

    Squash bottles and boxes before you carry them to a collection point.

  • Bring your own bag

    A reusable bag replaces hundreds of plastic ones over a year.
